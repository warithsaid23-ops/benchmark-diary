Engineer guide

Use the Daily Site Diary after each shift. Complete the required fields on every page, including all safety questions, the incident answer and sign-off.

  1. Enter the project, date and your name.
  2. Record workers, hours, weather, delays and the work completed.
  3. Select the programme status using the bilingual status button.
  4. Answer every safety question. Add details only when the question requires them.
  5. Review the report, complete the incident page and sign off.
  6. Submit once and keep the page open until it shows Submitted successfully.

If the connection is down, the report is saved on the phone and marked pending. Leave the page installed or open; it will retry when the connection returns. The retry uses the original report ID, so do not create a second report.

Drafts restore after reload. Photos are compressed and kept in the current phone draft; send the photos to the project team separately until photo storage is connected.
