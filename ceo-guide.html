CEO guide

Open the CEO dashboard to review today’s confirmed diary records. The dashboard uses the diary date, shows the latest successful sync time and keeps the last synced view available when offline.

  1. Use the project, engineer and programme filters to review records.
  2. Open a record to inspect work, delays, safety, materials, attendance, tasks and incidents.
  3. Use print or copy when a report needs to be shared.
  4. Check email status when the record shows a notification warning.

A row in the Sheet is the saving authority. A pending screen is not proof that the row exists. If a saved row has partial, failed or unknown email status, ask the administrator to inspect delivery; do not submit the same diary again.
